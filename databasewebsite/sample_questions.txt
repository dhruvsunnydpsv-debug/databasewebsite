The researcher noted that over a 30-year lifespan, a single tern may travel the equivalent of three round trips to the Moon. Which choice best states the main idea of the text?
A) Arctic terns are the most common migratory birds in the world.
B) Arctic terns travel extraordinary distances over the course of their lives.
C) Researchers have recently begun studying Arctic tern migration for the first time.
